## DISCOVERY SCRIPT
## (Inbound lead — first contact within 1 business day)

**Goal:** Qualify the lead, understand their situation, schedule the consultation.
**Time:** 10–15 minutes.
**Format:** Phone or text conversation.

---

**Opening:**

"Hi [Name], this is [Steve/Skeeter] from Veridian. You reached out about recovering revenue from missed calls — I wanted to connect personally and see if we can help. Is this a good time for a few quick questions?"

---

**Discovery Questions (pick 3–5 based on flow):**

1. "How many calls does your business receive in a typical month?"

2. "When a call comes in and nobody answers — what happens next? Do you have any system in place, or does it just go to voicemail?"

3. "What kind of work does your business do?" *(Confirm industry — security, contracting, medical, property management, legal)*

4. "If I told you that [X]% of the calls you miss never come back — does that feel accurate for your business?"

5. "Have you ever calculated how much you might be losing to missed calls? We have a calculator on our site — did you use it?" *(If yes: "What number did it show you?")*

6. "What's the average value of a new customer for you? *(Just ballpark — we use this to calculate your recovery potential.)*"

7. "Is it mostly that you can't get to the phone, or is it more that calls come in outside business hours?"

8. "Do you have someone who does follow-up with leads that don't convert right away, or does that fall through the cracks?"

---

**Qualify:**

Hot signals:
- Mentions specific dollar amounts they're losing
- Has already done the calculator
- "ASAP", "this is a problem right now", "we keep losing bids"
- High-value customers ($2K+), high miss rate (>25%)

Not-right-now signals:
- "I'm just exploring"
- Very low call volume (<20/month)
- Already has a system they're happy with

---

**Close to consultation:**

"Based on what you've told me, I think there's a real opportunity here. What I'd like to do is get you on a 30-minute call with me — no pitch, just your numbers. I'll show you exactly what we'd recover for your business and what it would look like. What's your schedule like this week?"

*(If they've already booked via the website: "I see you already booked a time — perfect. I'll come prepared with your numbers. Is [date/time] still good?")*

## CONSULTATION SCRIPT
## (30-minute consultation call — after discovery or direct booking)

**Goal:** Review their numbers live, present recovery potential, recommend a package, close to proposal.
**Pre-call prep:** Pull up their calculator data from dashboard. Print/review assessment if completed.

---

**Opening (2 min):**

"Thanks for making time. I want to use these 30 minutes to go through your numbers specifically — not a generic demo. I pulled up what you submitted and I have some thoughts. Fair warning: some of what I'm going to show you will be uncomfortable — in a good way."

---

**Review Their Numbers (8 min):**

If they used the calculator:
"You entered [X] monthly calls, [Y]% miss rate, and [$Z] average customer value. That means you're missing roughly [missed] calls per month. At your conversion rate, that's [opps] opportunities per month walking out the door. At [$val] per customer, that's [$lostMo] per month you're not capturing — [$annual] per year. Does that feel right to you?"

If no calculator data:
"Let me ask you a few numbers and we'll calculate it live together."
- Monthly calls: ___
- What percent go unanswered: ___%
- Average new customer value: $___
- What percent of inquiries convert to customers: ___%
→ Calculate: missed × conversion × value = monthly loss × 12 = annual loss

---

**Recovery Potential (5 min):**

"Based on what we see across businesses similar to yours, 68% of those missed calls can be recovered with the right response system. That's not guaranteed for your specific situation — but as a working number, that puts your recovery potential at about [$recMo]/month, or [$annual]/year."

"The question isn't whether you're losing money. You already calculated that. The question is: is this a problem you want to solve?"

---

**Show How It Works (8 min):**

"Here's what happens the moment a call goes unanswered with Veridian in place:"

Walk through the sequence (use the AutoDemo on the website, or describe verbally):
1. Call rings, nobody answers.
2. Within 60 seconds, Veridian sends a personalized message: "Hi, sorry we missed you — are you still looking for help?"
3. 68% of people respond. They tell us what they need.
4. We qualify them and schedule an appointment directly to your calendar.
5. You get notified. Your team takes over from there.

"This happens automatically. No staff action. No calls to return. No leads falling through the cracks."

---

**Address the Top Objections (3 min):**

**"Does this replace anyone on my team?"**
"No. It handles the calls that were already being lost — the ones nobody was getting to. Your team stays focused on the customers in front of them."

**"What if I don't have a lot of calls?"**
"Lower volume means every single call matters more. If you're only getting 40 calls a month and missing 15 of them, those 15 are all that stands between where you are and significant growth."

**"How long to get set up?"**
"Most clients are live within 5 business days. We handle everything. You make one call forwarding change."

**"What does it cost?"**
"It depends on your call volume and which services make sense for your situation. I'll send you a proposal after this call — it'll be specific to you, not a menu of options."

---

**Close (4 min):**

"Based on what you've told me, I'd recommend [Revenue Recovery / Customer Follow-Up / Growth Package]. It's designed for exactly your situation.

I'll send you a proposal by [specific day] — it'll include the specific services, the projected outcomes for your business, and the investment. You review it, ask me any questions, and we go from there.

Does that work for you?"

*(After yes)* "One thing before I let you go — is there anyone else at your company who'd be involved in a decision like this? I want to make sure the proposal addresses their questions too."

## CLIENT ONBOARDING CHECKLIST

**Use this for every new client. Complete in order.**

### Before Kickoff Call

- [ ] Confirm signed agreement on file
- [ ] Confirm payment method collected
- [ ] Create client record in dashboard (mark as "Won" in leads)
- [ ] Add client to GHL — tag: `client-active`
- [ ] Schedule kickoff call (Day 1 target)
- [ ] Send calendar invite with prep notes

### Kickoff Call Agenda (Day 1)

- [ ] Confirm business name, primary phone number, hours of operation
- [ ] Confirm call forwarding number and method
- [ ] Confirm response message tone (professional / friendly / formal)
- [ ] Confirm appointment booking preferences (days, times, lead time)
- [ ] Confirm escalation triggers (what makes a "hot" inbound lead)
- [ ] Confirm team contact for warm handoffs
- [ ] Confirm monthly report recipient email
- [ ] Walk through what they'll see on Day 4 (go-live walkthrough)

### Configuration (Day 2–3)

- [ ] Set up missed call detection for their number
- [ ] Configure first-response message template (use their name, their tone)
- [ ] Configure qualification questions for their industry
- [ ] Configure appointment booking slots and calendar sync
- [ ] Test full flow: missed call → response → qualification → booking
- [ ] Confirm test results with internal team
- [ ] Notify client: "You go live tomorrow — here's what to expect"

### Go Live (Day 4)

- [ ] Activate call forwarding (client makes the change, we confirm receipt)
- [ ] Monitor first 2–3 hours actively for any issues
- [ ] Confirm first response fires correctly on a live missed call
- [ ] Send client "You're live" message with what to watch for
- [ ] Log go-live date in dashboard

### Day 5+

- [ ] Schedule first monthly review (30 minutes, 30 days from go-live)
- [ ] Add to monthly reporting cycle
- [ ] Add to GHL pipeline: move to "Active Client" stage

## 30-DAY SUCCESS PLAN

**For new clients — share this at or just after go-live.**

---

**Your First 30 Days with Veridian**

Here's exactly what we're focused on for your first month.

**Week 1: Watch and Tune**
Your first real missed calls will be recovered. We'll watch every response and make sure the message tone and timing feel right. Expect a quick check-in from us at the end of Week 1.

**Week 2: First Results**
By Day 14, you'll have at least 2 weeks of data. We'll share a preview of what's been recovered so you can see the system working in real numbers.

**Week 3: Optimize**
If anything isn't converting the way we expect — response rate too low, wrong tone, missing a key question — we adjust now. Most clients see their first appointment booked from a recovered call by Day 14–21.

**Week 4: Monthly Review Prep**
We compile your first monthly report: missed calls, recovery rate, conversations started, appointments booked, estimated revenue impact. We review this together on your first monthly call.

**What you should do in the first 30 days:**
- When a warm lead is handed to you — follow up within 1 hour. Speed matters.
- If a recovered call doesn't convert — let us know. We want to understand why.
- If the response message doesn't sound right — tell us immediately. We fix it fast.

**What to expect at 30 days:**
- A clear before/after picture: missed call rate, recovery rate, appointments from recovery
- Your first monthly report in your inbox
- A 30-minute call to review results and decide what to optimize next

You'll know whether this is working within the first month. We stake our relationship on that.
