---

### Day 30 Monthly Review Agenda

**Pre-call prep:** Pull dashboard metrics before the call. Have the numbers ready.

**1. Results (10 min)**

Walk through these numbers for the month:
- Total calls handled
- Missed calls intercepted
- Response rate (people who replied to first message)
- Conversations started
- Appointments booked from recovery (if booking enabled)
- Estimated revenue recovered (calc: bookings × average customer value)

Framing: "Here's what happened this month — let me walk you through it."

**2. Quality Review (5 min)**

- Were there any conversations that felt off-brand?
- Any escalations that weren't handled correctly?
- Any complaints from callers about the messaging?
- Any suggestions from the client on tone or messaging?

Adjustments: make any changes live during the call if small, or confirm timeline if larger.

**3. Next Month Targets (3 min)**

- Are there changes to hours, availability, or service area coming?
- Are there promotions or campaigns we should personalize messaging around?
- Any new types of calls they're expecting?

**4. Close (2 min)**

"Based on what you've seen this month — is this working for your business?
Is there anything you'd change before next month?"

If the answer is no and they're happy: confirm auto-renewal or next billing cycle.
If they have concerns: address immediately. Do not leave concerns open.

## CLIENT HEALTH INDICATORS

Check these monthly. Flag any red signals immediately.

| Signal | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Response rate | > 60% | 40–60% | < 40% |
| Conversations started | Growing MoM | Flat | Declining |
| Client engagement | Replies to updates | Occasional | Silent |
| Warm handoff to close | Client following up | Inconsistent | No follow-up |
| Revenue recovered | > fee paid | 0.5–1× fee | < 0.5× fee |

**Red on revenue recovered** = at-risk account. Schedule a call immediately. Do not wait for the monthly review.
