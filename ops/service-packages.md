# Veridian — Service Packages
# Phase 3: Revenue Recovery | Customer Follow-Up | Growth Package

---

## PACKAGE 1: Revenue Recovery

**Positioning:** For businesses losing revenue to missed calls right now.

**Who it's for:**
- Service businesses with 50+ incoming calls/month
- Owners who are on-site and can't always answer
- Businesses with no automated response to missed calls

**What's included:**
- Missed call detection and response (< 60 seconds)
- Personalized first-touch text/email to every missed caller
- Lead qualification during the response conversation
- Appointment scheduling for qualified leads
- Monthly recovery report (missed vs. recovered, revenue impact)
- Dedicated account manager

**Outcome promised:**
Every missed call receives a professional response within 60 seconds. Qualified leads are moved toward an appointment without staff involvement.

**Success metric:**
Number of missed calls converted to conversations. Number of appointments generated from previously unanswered calls.

**Typical client:**
Security company, contractor, medical practice, or law firm with $50K–$500K in annual revenue at risk from missed calls.

---

## PACKAGE 2: Customer Follow-Up

**Positioning:** For businesses that have leads but lose them to slow or inconsistent follow-up.

**Who it's for:**
- Businesses with leads sitting in a CRM or inbox going stale
- Owners who know they should follow up but don't have time
- Teams where leads fall through the cracks between staff

**What's included:**
- Automated follow-up sequences triggered by missed calls or form submissions
- Multi-touch sequence: 24h, 3-day, 7-day, 14-day touchpoints
- Personalized messages using lead data (name, business, calculator numbers)
- Unsubscribe management and suppression
- Lead status tracking (proposal sent, won, monthly revenue)
- Monthly sequence performance report
- Dedicated account manager

**Outcome promised:**
Every lead receives consistent, personalized follow-up at the right intervals — without manual staff action. No lead goes cold from neglect.

**Success metric:**
Percentage of leads converted to consultations. Reduction in "stale lead" count. Appointments booked from follow-up sequence vs. direct contact.

**Typical client:**
Any service business where staff manually follows up (or forgets to), and where the sales cycle requires 2–4 touchpoints before a decision.

---

## PACKAGE 3: Growth Package

**Positioning:** Complete revenue recovery and follow-up system for businesses ready to scale.

**Who it's for:**
- Businesses that want both missed call recovery AND systematic follow-up
- Owners who want a complete front-end revenue system without hiring staff
- Businesses with $100K+ in estimated annual recovery potential

**What's included:**
Everything in Revenue Recovery + Everything in Customer Follow-Up, plus:
- GoHighLevel CRM integration (contacts, notes, opportunities, tags)
- Pipeline management setup (stages: New → Contacted → Proposal → Won)
- AI-generated recovery plan for new clients (using actual business data)
- Consultation booking integration
- Quarterly business review (30-minute call to review performance + optimize)
- Priority support (response within 4 business hours)
- Dedicated account manager

**Outcome promised:**
A complete, automated front-end that captures every missed call, follows up with every lead, and routes qualified prospects to your calendar — all without additional headcount.

**Success metric:**
Total revenue recovered from missed calls. Pipeline value in CRM. Conversion rate from inquiry to booked consultation.

**Typical client:**
Security company or contractor with $200K+ in annual revenue at risk, wanting a complete system to replace manual processes.

## Pricing Notes (Internal)

Pricing is not posted on the website. This is intentional — every client gets a proposal based on their call volume and recovery potential.

Suggested pricing framework:
- Revenue Recovery: Monthly retainer based on call volume tier
- Customer Follow-Up: Monthly retainer based on lead volume tier
- Growth Package: Bundled retainer, discounted vs. two packages separately

All packages start with a no-obligation consultation. Propose after reviewing their calculator data and assessment results.
