Our Story

Clean. Protect. Impress.

Viridian Pressure Wash was founded by Steve Smith on a simple belief: every property — home or business — deserves to look its best and be protected from the wear that Florida's climate brings. Backed by a service background rooted in professionalism and integrity, Steve built Viridian to bring that same standard to exterior cleaning.

Central Florida's humidity, pollen, and relentless algae growth cause homes and commercial properties to look dirty and worn within months — hurting curb appeal, property value, and first impressions. We exist to reverse that, safely and dependably.

Our Mission

To protect and elevate the value of every property we touch — using professional-grade equipment, eco-conscious cleaning methods, and meticulous attention to detail that homeowners and businesses can trust.

Our Vision

To become Central Florida's most trusted and recognized exterior cleaning brand — known for premium results, honest pricing, and service so reliable that our customers never call anyone else.

Why customers choose us

  • Surface-safe methods. We soft-wash delicate surfaces and pressure-wash hard ones — never the wrong tool for the job.
  • Eco-conscious detergents. Biodegradable cleaners, careful landscaping protection, and water-conscious technique.
  • Transparent pricing. Written estimates before any work begins — no surprises, no pressure.
  • Dependable scheduling. We show up on time, in uniform, and finish what we start.
