## 8. Google Posts (weekly cadence)

Rotate post types:
- **Offer:** "Spring House Wash Special — bundle a house wash + driveway and save."
- **Before/After:** photo + short caption + "Get your free quote."
- **Service spotlight:** "Roof streaks? Our soft wash removes algae safely."
- **Tip:** "Why Florida roofs get black streaks (and how to fix them)."
- **Service area:** "Now booking in {city} this week!"

Each post: 1 image, 1–2 sentences, a button (Call or Book), and a keyword + city.
