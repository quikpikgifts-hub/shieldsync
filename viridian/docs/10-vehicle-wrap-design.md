# Viridian Pressure Wash — Vehicle Wrap Design Spec

**Vehicle:** 2012 Mazda5 Grand Touring (factory light blue) · **Brand:** Viridian Pressure Wash · **Tagline:** Clean. Protect. Impress.

> **About these mockups:** the files in `/wrap` are **clean vector schematics** (SVG) showing layout, copy, color, and element placement for all five views. They are designed to hand to a professional wrap shop as a design brief — they are *not* photo-composites onto your actual car photos. A wrap installer will take these specs, your exact vehicle measurements/template, and produce print-ready, panel-accurate artwork. (I can't generate photoreal renders, and a real install needs the manufacturer's vehicle template anyway.)

## 1. Design Goals

Modern · Clean · Highly visible · Professional · Premium · **Easy to read while driving** · Memorable & trustworthy. The wrap turns the Mazda5 into a moving billboard in every neighborhood it serves.

---

## 2. Color & Base

- **Base:** Deep Navy `#0B1F3A` over the lower two-thirds of the body (covers/complements the factory light blue).
- **Sweep:** a dynamic **green-to-aqua water wave** (`#0C6B3A` → `#1FA7E1`) sweeping front-low to rear-high, echoing the logo.
- **Upper body / roof pillars:** kept lighter (white/light blue) so the wrap reads cleanly and isn't too heavy.
- **Splash accents:** subtle white/aqua water-splash texture near the wave for energy (used sparingly).

---

## 3. Element Placement

### Driver & Passenger Sides (primary real estate)
- **Large "V" logo lockup** centered on the rear sliding-door/quarter panel.
- **VIRIDIAN PRESSURE WASH** wordmark large across the doors; **tagline** beneath.
- **Services list** (bulleted, high-contrast):
  - House Washing · Driveway Cleaning · Patios · Sidewalks · Roof Soft Wash · Commercial Services
- **Phone** `407-555-0123` and **website** `ViridianWash.com` — large, legible at distance.
- **QR code placeholder** (links to the quote page) near the rear door.
- **Social icons** (Facebook, Instagram) near the logo.

### Rear (hatch — read in traffic)
- Centered **logo + wordmark**, **phone**, **website**, and **QR code**, sized for the car behind you to read at a stoplight. Minimal clutter.

### Front (hood / bumper — head-on visibility)
- Smaller **logo/wordmark** for parking-lot and rear-view-mirror recognition. Keep clear of sensors/badges.

### Top (roof — aerial/drone marketing)
- Bold **"V" mark** + short wordmark for drone/overhead photography and tall-building visibility.

---

## 4. Readability Rules (drive-by legibility)

1. **Phone & website are the most important info** — make them the largest text after the brand name.
2. High contrast only: white/aqua type on navy; navy type on white. No low-contrast combos.
3. Limit body copy — a moving viewer has ~3 seconds. Brand + 1 line + phone + website + QR.
4. Keep critical text **above the wheel arches and door handles**, away from seams and curved panels where it distorts.
5. Bold weights (Montserrat ExtraBold) for all distance-read text.

---

## 5. Production Notes for the Wrap Shop

- Request the **2012 Mazda5 wrap template** (exact panel dimensions) before finalizing artwork.
- Provide vector logo files (outlined fonts) and the palette HEX values from the brand guide.
- Account for **door handles, gas cap, seams, sliding-door tracks, and glass** — keep key text off them.
- Decide **full wrap vs. partial wrap + cut vinyl** based on budget (partial can achieve ~80% of the impact for less).
- Use **cast vinyl + laminate** for durability under Florida sun; expect 5–7 year life with care.
- Generate the **QR code** to point to `https://viridianwash.com/contact.html` (or a tracked URL) and test-scan before printing.
