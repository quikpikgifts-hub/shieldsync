# Viridian Pressure Wash — Business Plan

**Clean. Protect. Impress.**
Owner: Steve Smith · Sanford, Florida · Mobile Pressure Washing & Exterior Cleaning

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Viridian Pressure Wash is a locally owned, mobile exterior-cleaning company based in Sanford, Florida, serving residential and commercial customers across the greater Central Florida market (Seminole, Volusia, and Orange counties). The company delivers professional house washing, driveway and surface cleaning, roof soft washing, and commercial pressure washing using surface-safe, eco-conscious methods.

The Central Florida climate — high humidity, heavy pollen, and year-round algae growth — causes properties to deteriorate visually within months, creating consistent, recurring demand. Viridian differentiates on a premium, modern brand, transparent published pricing, and the discipline and integrity of an owner with a service/security background.

The business launches lean: a single owner-operator using a wrapped 2012 Mazda5 Grand Touring and professional-grade equipment, targeting profitability within the first 90 days and a path to a second crew/vehicle by year-end. Startup costs are modest (~$6,000–$9,000), margins are strong (60–75% gross on residential jobs), and the recurring commercial contract model creates predictable base revenue.

**The ask / objective:** Build a launch-ready, self-funded (or lightly financed) operation that begins booking jobs immediately and reaches $60k–$120k in first-year revenue depending on growth scenario.

## 5. Market Analysis

**Industry.** The U.S. pressure washing services market is large, fragmented, and growing, driven by home-improvement spending, real estate turnover, and commercial property maintenance. Most operators are small, owner-run businesses with weak branding.

**Local demand drivers (Central Florida):**
- Subtropical humidity + frequent rain → rapid algae, mildew, and mold growth on roofs, siding, driveways, and pavers.
- Heavy seasonal pollen → visible surface staining.
- Strong housing market and HOA density → recurring residential and community demand.
- Tourism-adjacent commercial corridors (Orlando, Sanford) → storefront and restaurant maintenance needs.

**Target customers:**
1. **Residential homeowners** — single-family homes, 1–3 stories, in Sanford, Lake Mary, Longwood, Oviedo, Winter Springs, Deltona, DeBary, Apopka, and Orlando.
2. **HOA communities** — recurring driveway, sidewalk, monument, and common-area cleaning.
3. **Commercial property managers** — retail strips, restaurants, offices, apartments.
4. **Real estate agents** — pre-listing curb-appeal cleans.

**Market size (illustrative).** A single owner-operator can realistically service 15–25 residential jobs/month at maturity. Capturing even a fraction of one ZIP code's homes on an annual cleaning cycle supports a full schedule.

## 7. SWOT Analysis

**Strengths**
- Low overhead, high-margin service
- Premium brand + wrapped vehicle marketing
- Transparent pricing and professional process
- Owner discipline and customer-trust positioning

**Weaknesses**
- Single operator initially (capacity ceiling)
- Compact vehicle (Mazda5) limits water/equipment hauling vs. a truck/trailer
- New brand with no review history at launch
- Weather-dependent scheduling

**Opportunities**
- Recurring HOA and commercial contracts for stable revenue
- Realtor partnerships for pre-listing cleans
- Seasonal upsells (holiday light prep, pre-holiday cleans)
- Expansion to second crew/vehicle and trailer rig

**Threats**
- Low barrier to entry → new competitors
- Price-cutting "weekend" operators
- Equipment downtime / repair costs
- Florida rainy season scheduling disruption

## 14. Three-Year Growth Plan

- **Year 1:** Establish brand, reviews, and a reliable residential pipeline; secure first recurring commercial contracts. Owner-operator + occasional helper.
- **Year 2:** Add a second vehicle/crew and a trailer rig (larger water capacity). Formalize recurring HOA/PM contracts as 30–40% of revenue. Target $150k–$250k.
- **Year 3:** 2–3 crews, a dedicated estimator/booking process, and a recognizable regional brand. Target $300k–$500k with commercial contracts as the revenue floor.

## 15. Five-Year Growth Plan

Build Viridian into a multi-crew regional exterior-cleaning company with diversified revenue (residential, recurring commercial, and seasonal services), standardized operations, hired and trained technicians, and a defensible local brand. Optional add-on lines: window cleaning, paver sealing, and holiday lighting. Target $500k–$1M+ with the owner transitioning from technician to operator/manager.

## 16. Exit Strategy

Options, in order of likelihood:
1. **Lifestyle business / owner income** — run indefinitely as a profitable owner-managed company.
2. **Acquisition** — sell to a larger home-services or franchise consolidator; recurring commercial contracts and review reputation drive valuation (typically a multiple of seller's discretionary earnings).
3. **Owner transition** — hire a general manager and retain ownership as a semi-absentee investor.
4. **Asset sale / wind-down** — sell equipment, vehicle, brand, customer list, and contracts.

To maximize exit value: document SOPs, build recurring contract revenue, maintain clean books, and protect the brand and online reviews.
