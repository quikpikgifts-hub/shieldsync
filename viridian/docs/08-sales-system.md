# Viridian Pressure Wash — Sales System

Scripts, templates, and sequences to turn inquiries into booked, paid, reviewed, and repeat customers. Keep the tone professional, helpful, and honest — never pushy.

---

## 1. Phone Script (inbound quote call)

**Greeting:** "Thanks for calling Viridian Pressure Wash, this is Steve. How can I help you today?"

**Discover (ask, then listen):**
- "Great — is this for your home or a business?"
- "Which surfaces are we looking at — driveway, house, roof, patio…?"
- "Roughly how big is the area / how many stories?"
- "What's the address so I can factor in your area and water access?"
- "When were you hoping to get this done?"

**Position value:**
> "We use surface-safe soft washing where it matters, eco-conscious detergents, and we're fully licensed and insured. You'll get a written estimate up front — no surprises."

**Quote & close:**
> "For a {service} like that, you're looking at about {range}. I can lock in {day/time} and send you a written estimate to confirm. Does {Tuesday morning} work?"

**If price objection:**
> "Totally understand. We're not the cheapest, but we're insured, careful around your landscaping, and we stand behind the results. I can also bundle your {driveway} with the {house wash} to bring the total down — want me to price that?"

**Close:** "I'll text you a confirmation and the written estimate now. Looking forward to it!"

## 2. Text Message Templates

**Quick quote reply (from web form/missed call):**
> "Hi {Name}, this is Steve with Viridian Pressure Wash 👋 Thanks for reaching out about {service}! Mind sharing your address and a couple photos? I'll send a written estimate today."

**Appointment confirmation:**
> "Confirmed ✅ Viridian Pressure Wash will be at {address} on {day} between {window}. Please clear vehicles/items from the area and make sure an outdoor spigot is accessible. Questions? Just reply here."

**On-the-way:**
> "Hi {Name}, Steve here — on my way, ETA ~{time}. See you soon!"

**Job complete:**
> "All done at {address}! 🚿 Hope you love the results. Your invoice is attached/linked here: {link}. Thanks for choosing Viridian — Clean. Protect. Impress."

## 4. Follow-Up Sequences

**Lead that didn't book (no response):**
1. **Day 0:** Send estimate (text + email).
2. **Day 2:** "Hi {Name}, just checking in on the estimate I sent — happy to answer any questions or adjust the scope."
3. **Day 5:** "Still interested? I have a couple openings {timeframe} and can hold one for you."
4. **Day 10:** "I'll close out this quote for now — reach out anytime and I'll honor the same pricing. Thanks!"

**Post-job sequence:**
1. **Day 0:** Job-complete text + invoice.
2. **Day 1:** Review request (text or email — see templates).
3. **Day 3:** If no review, gentle reminder + mention $20 referral credit.
4. **Month 6/12:** Seasonal reminder: "It's been a while — Florida grime builds up fast. Want to get back on the schedule?"

---

## 5. Estimate Template (fields)

- Business header (logo, phone, website, license/insurance note)
- Customer name, address, date, estimate #
- Line items: service, surface, area/qty, price
- Bundle discount line
- **Total** + validity ("estimate valid 30 days")
- Notes: surface-safe method, what's included, access requirements
- Acceptance line (signature / "reply YES to book")

---

## 6. Invoice Template (fields)

- Business header + invoice #
- Customer + service address + date completed
- Line items + total
- Payment methods (card, Zelle, check) + due date ("due on receipt")
- Thank-you + review link + referral offer
- Tax line (per CPA guidance)

> Use Jobber/Housecall Pro or a simple branded PDF/Wave invoice to automate these once volume grows.

## 8. Referral Scripts

**Verbal (at job completion):** "By the way — if you refer a neighbor, you both get $20 off. A lot of our work comes from word of mouth, and we really appreciate it."

**Text:** "Know a neighbor whose driveway or roof could use some love? Refer them to Viridian and you'll *both* get $20 off. Just have them mention your name. 🙌"

---

## 9. Sales Cadence Summary

Inquiry → same-day written estimate → confirmation text → on-the-way text → job complete + invoice → review request (Day 1) → referral ask → seasonal re-engagement (6/12 mo). Automate as much as possible once you adopt a CRM.
