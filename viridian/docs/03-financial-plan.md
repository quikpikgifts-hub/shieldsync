## 4. Break-Even Analysis

- **Fixed monthly costs:** ~$825
- **Variable cost per job:** ~12% of ticket (chemicals, fuel, wear)
- **Contribution margin per average $250 job:** $250 − $30 ≈ **$220**

**Break-even jobs/month** = $825 ÷ $220 ≈ **4 jobs/month**

> Viridian covers its fixed costs at roughly **one job per week**. Everything beyond ~4 jobs/month is profit (before owner taxes and equipment amortization). Including the startup investment (~$7,500), full payback occurs after roughly **35–40 average jobs** — typically **2–4 months** in the Medium scenario.

## 8. Financial Discipline Rules

1. Set aside **25–30%** of net profit for taxes every month.
2. Keep a **3-month operating reserve** before drawing a full owner salary.
3. Reinvest early profits into **marketing** and **equipment redundancy** (a second pump prevents lost days).
4. Don't compete on price — protect the **mid-premium** position and margin.
5. Review pricing after a **full season** of real cost/job data.
