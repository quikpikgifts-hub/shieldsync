# Viridian Pressure Wash — Equipment & Vehicle Setup Plan

Vehicle: **2012 Mazda5 Grand Touring** (light blue) — a compact 6-passenger MPV. With the rear seats folded, it offers a flat, enclosed cargo area suitable for a lean mobile pressure-washing rig. This plan maximizes that space safely and lays out the path to a trailer upgrade.

> ⚠️ **Reality check on the Mazda5:** it's a compact minivan, not a cargo van or truck. Payload is limited (roughly ~1,000 lbs including passengers). Water is heavy (~8.34 lbs/gal), so keep onboard water modest (a small buck/tank, ~25–50 gal max) and rely on the customer's spigot for supply where possible. Plan the trailer/truck upgrade as volume grows.

---

## 1. Core Equipment List

| Category | Item | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Power | **3,500 PSI gas pressure washer** (≈2.5+ GPM) | Primary unit; gas = no outlet needed |
| Surface prep | **Surface cleaner** (15–20") | Even, fast flatwork (driveways, patios) |
| Soft wash | Down-stream injector / X-Jet **or** 12V soft-wash system | Low-pressure chemical application for siding/roofs |
| Hose | High-pressure hose (100–150 ft) + garden feed hose (100 ft) | Reach without moving the vehicle |
| Reels | Hose reels (pressure + chemical) | Tangle-free, faster setup/teardown |
| Guns/wands | Spray gun, wand/lance, trigger gun | |
| Nozzles | Nozzle set (0°, 15°, 25°, 40°, soap) + turbo nozzle | Right pattern per surface |
| Application | Pump-up sprayers (2) | Spot treatment, pre-treat |
| Chemicals | SH (sodium hypochlorite), surfactant, degreaser, concrete brightener | Stored in **labeled, sealed** containers, secured upright |
| Water | Compact water tank/buck (~25–50 gal) + intake/float | Buffer; supplement customer supply |
| Safety | Gloves, eye protection, non-slip boots, respirator, ear protection | PPE |
| Storage | Stackable storage bins, tie-down straps, spill tray | Contain leaks, organize |
| Tools | Tool kit, spare parts (o-rings, fuses, fittings, nozzles), funnel | Field repairs |
| Misc | First-aid kit, traffic cones (2–4), wheel chocks, drop cloth | Job-site safety |

## 2. Vehicle Cargo Layout (Mazda5, rear seats folded)

```
        FRONT (driver / passenger)
 ┌───────────────────────────────────────┐
 │  [ Driver ]        [ Passenger ]       │
 ├───────────────────────────────────────┤
 │  2nd ROW (keep 1 seat for helper)      │
 │  [ helper seat ]   [ folded flat ]     │
 ├───────────────────────────────────────┤
 │  CARGO ZONE A (over rear axle)         │
 │  ▓ Pressure washer (heaviest) ▓        │  ← weight over/ahead of rear axle
 │  ▓ Water buck/tank (secured)  ▓        │
 ├───────────────────────────────────────┤
 │  CARGO ZONE B                          │
 │  □ Hose reels   □ Surface cleaner      │
 │  □ Chemical bin (upright, spill tray)  │
 ├───────────────────────────────────────┤
 │  CARGO ZONE C (rear hatch)             │
 │  □ Bins: nozzles/tools/PPE  □ Cones    │  ← lightest, most-accessed items
 └───────────────────────────────────────┘
              REAR HATCH (load/unload)
```

**Principles**
- **Heaviest items (pressure washer, water) low and over/ahead of the rear axle** for stability and to protect payload limits.
- **Chemicals upright, sealed, in a spill tray**, separated from electronics and away from the cabin; ventilate.
- **Frequently used items (nozzles, PPE, cones) near the hatch** for quick access.
- **Everything strapped/tied down** — no loose heavy objects behind passengers.
- **Protect the interior** with a rubber liner/tarp; chemicals and water will find every seam.

## 3. Storage Solutions

- **Stackable, sealable bins** labeled by category (Nozzles & Fittings, PPE, Chemicals, Tools, Spares).
- **Hose reels mounted** to a low plywood base/board that sits in the cargo well (removable).
- **Chemical caddy** with secondary containment (spill tray) and absorbent pads.
- **Door/seat-back organizers** for small frequently-grabbed items.
- **Roof rack (optional)** for light, bulky, weather-safe items (e.g., extension wand, ladder) to free cabin space — mind height and aerodynamics.

---

## 4. Weight Distribution Plan

1. Estimate total load: pressure washer (~60–80 lb) + ~25–50 gal water (~210–420 lb) + chemicals + gear + helper. Keep **total occupants + cargo within payload**.
2. Place the **water buck and pressure washer centered, low, and just ahead of the rear axle.**
3. Avoid loading everything at the very rear hatch (causes sag, poor handling, headlight aim issues).
4. **Travel with minimal water** when the customer has spigot access; fill only what you need.
5. Check **tire pressure** regularly (loaded) and keep the vehicle serviced.

---

## 5. Trailer / Truck Expansion Plan

When the Mazda5 caps your capacity (no-water-access jobs, larger commercial work, or 2-person crews), upgrade in stages:

**Stage 1 — Small enclosed/utility trailer (Year 1–2)**
- 5×8 or 6×10 trailer pulled by the Mazda5 (verify tow rating — Mazda5 towing is light, ~1,500 lb; a small single-axle trailer with a modest tank only).
- Mount a **larger water tank (100–225 gal)**, hose reels, and a generator if needed.

**Stage 2 — Dedicated work truck/van + trailer (Year 2–3)**
- Move to a **pickup or cargo van** that can safely haul a 200–325 gal tank, hot-water unit, and reels.
- Build a proper **skid rig** (pump, tank, reels on a frame) for fast loading.

**Stage 3 — Multi-rig fleet (Year 3+)**
- Second wrapped vehicle/trailer for a second crew; standardize each rig identically so any tech can run any truck.

---

## 6. Maintenance & Redundancy

- Daily: rinse equipment, check oil, inspect hoses/fittings, refill fuel.
- Weekly: pump check, nozzle inspection, chemical inventory.
- Carry a **spare parts kit** (o-rings, fuses, nozzles, quick-connects) — a $5 part can otherwise cost a lost day.
- Budget a **repair reserve** (see financial plan); consider a backup pump as revenue grows — downtime = lost jobs.
- Keep **Safety Data Sheets (SDS)** for all chemicals in the vehicle.
