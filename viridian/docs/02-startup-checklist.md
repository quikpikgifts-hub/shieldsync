# Viridian Pressure Wash — Startup Checklist

A step-by-step, launch-ready checklist. Work top to bottom; most items can run in parallel during Weeks 1–4.

---

## 1. Business Registration
- [ ] Choose final legal name & confirm availability (Florida Division of Corporations, **Sunbiz.org**)
- [ ] File **Articles of Organization** for a single-member LLC (Viridian Pressure Wash LLC)
- [ ] Designate a registered agent (self or service)
- [ ] Obtain **EIN** from the IRS (free, irs.gov — 10 minutes online)
- [ ] File a **fictitious name (DBA)** if operating under a name different from the LLC
- [ ] Register for Florida sales tax with the **FL Dept. of Revenue** if selling taxable services/goods (confirm taxability of cleaning services with a local CPA)

## 2. Licensing
- [ ] Obtain a local **Business Tax Receipt (BTR)** for the city of Sanford and Seminole County
- [ ] Confirm whether jobs in Orange/Volusia counties (Orlando, Deltona, DeBary) require separate BTRs
- [ ] Verify pressure-washing / wastewater-runoff rules with the municipality (many areas restrict where wash water may drain)
- [ ] No state contractor license is generally required for exterior cleaning — **confirm locally**

## 3. Insurance
- [ ] **General liability** policy — $1M+ per occurrence (required for most commercial/HOA contracts)
- [ ] Add **care, custody & control** coverage (protects against damage to the property you're cleaning)
- [ ] **Commercial auto** policy/endorsement for the work vehicle
- [ ] **Inland marine / equipment** coverage for the pressure washer and gear
- [ ] **Workers' comp** when you hire your first employee (required in FL at the applicable threshold)
- [ ] Save a **Certificate of Insurance (COI)** template to send commercial clients on request

## 4. Banking & Finance
- [ ] Open a dedicated **business checking account** (EIN required)
- [ ] Open a **business savings** account for taxes (set aside ~25–30% of net)
- [ ] Get a **business debit/credit card** to keep expenses separate
- [ ] Set up **bookkeeping** (QuickBooks, Wave, or a spreadsheet to start)
- [ ] Set up **payment acceptance**: card (Square/Stripe), Zelle, check, and online invoicing
- [ ] Find a local **CPA/tax pro** for quarterly estimated taxes and year-end filing

## 5. Equipment Purchases
*(full detail in 09-equipment-vehicle-setup.md)*
- [ ] 3,500 PSI gas pressure washer
- [ ] Surface cleaner attachment
- [ ] Soft-wash setup (pump/down-stream injector, X-Jet, or 12V system)
- [ ] Hoses, hose reels, spray guns, nozzle set, quick-connects
- [ ] Pump-up sprayers & chemical containers (clearly labeled)
- [ ] Cleaning chemicals (SH/sodium hypochlorite, surfactant, degreaser, brightener)
- [ ] Water tank/buck (compact, Mazda5-appropriate) + intake setup
- [ ] Safety equipment: gloves, eye protection, non-slip boots, respirator
- [ ] Storage bins, tool kit, first-aid kit, traffic cones
- [ ] Backup parts kit (fuses, o-rings, nozzles, fittings)

## 6. Vehicle Setup
- [ ] Service the **2012 Mazda5 Grand Touring** (oil, brakes, tires, fluids) before launch
- [ ] Install cargo organization (bins, tie-downs, protective liner) — see cargo layout doc
- [ ] **Wrap the vehicle** (see 10-vehicle-wrap-design.md) — logo, phone, website, services, social, QR
- [ ] Plan **weight distribution** so the vehicle stays within payload limits
- [ ] Document the trailer-expansion plan for when capacity is exceeded

## 7. Branding
- [ ] Finalize **logo** files (full color, white/reverse, icon-only) — see 04-branding-guide.md
- [ ] Lock **color palette** and **fonts** (Montserrat / Open Sans)
- [ ] Order **uniforms** (branded polos/tees + hat)
- [ ] Order **business cards**, **yard signs**, and **door hangers**
- [ ] Create branded **estimate** and **invoice** templates (see 08-sales-system.md)
- [ ] Set up **business email** (hello@viridianwash.com) and a **business phone line** (Google Voice or carrier)

## 8. Website & Online Presence
- [ ] Register domain (**ViridianWash.com**) and deploy this website
- [ ] Connect a real **quote/contact form** backend (Formspree, Netlify Forms, or CRM)
- [ ] Set up **Google Business Profile** (see 06-google-business-profile.md) and verify
- [ ] Create **Facebook**, **Instagram**, **TikTok**, and **Nextdoor** business profiles
- [ ] Add **click-to-call** and consistent **NAP** (Name/Address/Phone) everywhere

## 9. Marketing Launch
*(full plan in 07-marketing-plan.md)*
- [ ] Print and distribute **door hangers** in target neighborhoods
- [ ] Place a **yard sign** at every completed job (with permission)
- [ ] Post **before/after** content to social + Google Business Profile
- [ ] Stand up a **referral program** ($20 credit both ways)
- [ ] Launch a small **local Facebook/Instagram ad** budget once you have photos

## 10. Customer Acquisition (first 30 days)
- [ ] Offer a **launch discount** to the first 10 customers in exchange for reviews
- [ ] Ask **every** happy customer for a Google review (see review request templates)
- [ ] Door-knock or flyer the **two streets adjacent** to every completed job
- [ ] Collect email/phone for follow-up and seasonal reminders

## 11. Commercial Sales
- [ ] Build a **one-page rate sheet** for property managers/HOAs
- [ ] List target accounts: retail strips, restaurants, gas stations, apartment complexes, HOAs
- [ ] Direct outreach (call + email + drop-by) using the phone/email scripts (see 08-sales-system.md)
- [ ] Offer **recurring monthly/quarterly** maintenance contracts
- [ ] Keep COIs and W-9 ready for vendor onboarding

## 12. Growth & Scaling
- [ ] Implement **scheduling/CRM software** once volume justifies it (Jobber, Housecall Pro)
- [ ] Hire a **part-time helper** for two-person commercial jobs
- [ ] Track **cost per lead** and **close rate** by channel; double down on winners
- [ ] Reassess pricing after a **full season** of real job data
- [ ] Plan **second vehicle/trailer rig** when the calendar is consistently full
