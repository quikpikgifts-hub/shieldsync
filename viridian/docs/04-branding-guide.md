# Viridian Pressure Wash — Brand Identity Guide

**Clean. Protect. Impress.**

---

## 1. Brand Essence

Viridian is **professional, dependable, trustworthy, modern, and premium** — exterior cleaning with the discipline and integrity of a service/security background. Every touchpoint (vehicle, uniform, website, invoice) should feel clean, confident, and meticulous.

**Brand personality:** Professional · Dependable · Trustworthy · Modern · Premium · Technology-Driven · Attention to Detail · Community-Focused

**Voice & tone:** Clear, confident, helpful, and honest. No hype, no jargon. We explain the *why* (surface safety, eco-conscious methods) and we never pressure. Friendly but buttoned-up.

---

## 2. Logo

**Concept.** A stylized **"V"** formed as a sharp upward checkmark/wave in green-to-aqua, paired with a bold navy wordmark. The wave ties the mark to water and cleaning; the sharp angles communicate precision and professionalism.

**Files to produce** (`/assets`):
- `logo.svg` — full-color primary (included)
- Full-color horizontal lockup (mark + wordmark)
- Stacked lockup (mark over wordmark) for square spaces
- One-color **white/reverse** version for dark backgrounds & the wrap
- **Icon-only** "V" mark for favicons, social avatars, and the vehicle hood/roof

**Clear space:** keep padding around the logo equal to the height of the "V" mark. **Minimum size:** wordmark legible at ~120px wide on screen / 1" in print.

**Don'ts:** don't stretch, recolor outside the palette, add drop shadows/outlines, place the color logo on a busy photo without a scrim, or rotate the mark.

---

## 3. Color Palette

| Swatch | Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ◼ | Deep Navy | `#0B1F3A` | 11, 31, 58 | Primary text, headers, wrap base, dark sections |
| ◼ | Viridian Green | `#0C6B3A` | 12, 107, 58 | Primary brand color, buttons, accents |
| ◼ | Aqua Blue | `#1FA7E1` | 31, 167, 225 | Secondary accent, water/wave elements, links |
| ◻ | White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Backgrounds, reverse logo, negative space |
| ◻ | Light Gray | `#E0E0E0` | 224, 224, 224 | Borders, dividers, subtle backgrounds |

**Usage ratio (roughly):** 60% white/light, 25% navy, 10% green, 5% aqua. Green is the "action" color (CTAs); aqua is the "water" accent; navy is authority/trust.

---

## 4. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headings | **Montserrat** | ExtraBold (800) | Geometric, modern, confident |
| Subheadings | **Montserrat** | SemiBold (600) | |
| Body | **Open Sans** | Regular/SemiBold (400/600) | Highly legible at all sizes |

Web fonts load via Google Fonts (already wired in the site). For print/wrap, embed or outline the fonts. Fallback stack: `Montserrat, Arial, sans-serif` and `Open Sans, system-ui, Arial, sans-serif`.

---

## 5. Imagery & Photography

- **Before/after pairs** are the hero of the brand — shoot from the same angle, same framing.
- Bright, clean, high-contrast outdoor shots; show wet/clean surfaces and the wrapped vehicle on location.
- Avoid stock-photo clichés; real local jobs build trust.
- Add subtle water-splash / wave motifs sparingly to echo the logo.

---

## 6. Brand Applications

- **Vehicle wrap:** navy base, green/aqua wave, large "V" mark, services list, phone, website, QR, social icons (see 10-vehicle-wrap-design.md).
- **Uniform:** navy or white branded polo/tee + cap with the icon mark.
- **Stationery:** estimate & invoice templates with logo, palette, and tagline (see 08-sales-system.md).
- **Yard signs / door hangers:** white background, navy headline, green CTA, before/after image.
- **Digital:** website, Google Business Profile, and social all use the same logo, colors, and NAP.

---

## 7. Tagline & Messaging

- **Primary tagline:** *Clean. Protect. Impress.*
- **Alternates:** "A cleaner property. A better impression." · "Exterior cleaning done right."
- **Elevator pitch:** "Viridian Pressure Wash is Sanford's professional, eco-conscious exterior cleaning company — we restore curb appeal and protect your property's value with surface-safe soft washing and transparent pricing."
- **Proof points to repeat everywhere:** Licensed & Insured · Surface-Safe Soft Wash · Transparent Pricing · Locally Owned.
