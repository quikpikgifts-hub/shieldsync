## 3. Meta Descriptions

Each page ships a unique, benefit-driven meta description (140–160 chars) with a call-to-action and phone number. Pattern:

> "{Service} in {city} and Central Florida. {Benefit}. Free written estimates — call 407-555-0123."

---

## 4. Schema Markup (structured data)

- **LocalBusiness** schema on the Home page (implemented): name, phone, email, address locality, `areaServed`, hours, price range, slogan.
- **FAQPage** schema on the FAQs page (implemented) — eligible for rich results.
- **Service** schema (add per service page) describing each offering.
- **Review/AggregateRating** schema once you have verified reviews (don't fabricate — add when real).
- **BreadcrumbList** schema matching the on-page breadcrumbs.

> Validate with Google's Rich Results Test before/after launch.

---

## 5. On-Page SEO Rules

- One `<h1>` per page containing the primary keyword.
- Descriptive `<h2>/<h3>` with secondary keywords; natural language, no stuffing.
- Image `alt` text describing the subject + location ("driveway cleaning Sanford FL").
- Fast loading: the site is static, inline-light, and font-preconnected — keep images compressed (WebP) and lazy-loaded.
- Mobile-first (already responsive) — Google indexes mobile.
- Internal links between Home → Services → Residential/Commercial → Pricing → Contact.
- Consistent **NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) in the footer and on Contact, matching Google Business Profile exactly.

---

## 6. Service Pages Strategy

Create dedicated, keyword-targeted pages over time:
- One page per **core service** (house washing, driveway, roof soft wash, commercial).
- One **city landing page** per priority market (e.g. "Pressure Washing Lake Mary FL") with localized copy, a map, and local reviews. This is the biggest organic growth lever for multi-city service businesses.

---

## 7. Blog Strategy

Publish **1–2 posts/month** targeting informational + local intent. Starter calendar (titles already seeded on the Blog page):
1. Why Soft Washing Is the Right Choice for Florida Roofs
2. How Often Should You Pressure Wash Your Home in Central Florida?
3. 5 Exterior Cleaning Wins Before You List Your Home
4. What HOAs Should Look for in a Pressure Washing Contract
5. Driveway Cleaning: Pressure Washing vs. Surface Cleaning
6. Pollen Season in Central Florida: Protecting Your Home's Exterior

Each post: 700–1,200 words, one target keyword, internal links to the relevant service + Contact, and a CTA.

---

## 8. Internal Linking Plan

- Home links to all top services + Contact.
- Each service page links to Pricing, Contact, and 1–2 related services.
- Blog posts link to the most relevant service page and to Contact.
- Footer links to popular services and service areas on every page (implemented).

---

## 9. Off-Page / Local SEO

- **Google Business Profile** (see 06) — the #1 local ranking factor; optimize and post weekly.
- **Reviews** — steady flow of Google reviews with keywords/locations naturally included.
- **Local citations** — consistent NAP on Yelp, Bing Places, Nextdoor, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Apple Maps, and Facebook.
- **Backlinks** — local partnerships (realtors, HOAs), Chamber of Commerce, and supplier directories.

---

## 10. Measurement

- Set up **Google Search Console** + **Google Analytics 4** (or a privacy-friendly analytics tool).
- Track: organic impressions/clicks by keyword, GBP calls/direction requests, form submissions, and rankings for the primary keyword set.
- Review monthly; double down on the city + service pages that gain traction.
