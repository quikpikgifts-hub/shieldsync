# Viridian Pressure Wash — Marketing Plan (30 / 60 / 90 Day)

Objective: generate a consistent flow of quote requests and convert them into reviews and recurring contracts. The strategy stacks **local SEO + Google Business Profile + neighborhood saturation + social proof**, with the wrapped vehicle as a 24/7 billboard.

---

## Channels at a Glance

| Channel | Role | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Map Pack, calls, "near me" | Free |
| Vehicle wrap | Local awareness everywhere you drive/park | One-time |
| Door hangers / flyers | Neighborhood saturation around jobs | Low |
| Facebook / Instagram | Before/after social proof + paid ads | Low–Med |
| TikTok | Satisfying before/after reels (reach) | Free |
| Nextdoor | Hyper-local recommendations | Free–Low |
| Yard signs | Proof + leads at every job site | Low |
| Referral program | Word-of-mouth multiplier | Variable |
| Realtor / PM / HOA outreach | Recurring & high-ticket B2B | Time |

---

## 30-Day Launch Strategy

**Goal:** be findable, get the first 10 jobs and reviews, look established.

- [ ] Launch website + connect the quote form to a real backend
- [ ] Set up & verify **Google Business Profile**; add photos, services, posts
- [ ] Create **Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Nextdoor** business profiles (consistent NAP + logo)
- [ ] Wrap the vehicle; park it visibly when not on jobs
- [ ] Print **door hangers, flyers, yard signs, business cards**
- [ ] **Launch offer:** discounted service for the first 10 customers in exchange for an honest Google review
- [ ] Distribute door hangers to **2–3 target neighborhoods** (start in higher-value HOAs near Sanford/Lake Mary)
- [ ] Post **before/after** content 3×/week across social + a Google Post weekly
- [ ] Ask **every** customer for a review; reply to all
- [ ] Tell friends/family/network the business is open (organic first jobs)

**30-day KPIs:** GBP verified, 10+ jobs, 8+ reviews, 200+ door hangers out, profiles live.

---

## 60-Day Marketing Plan

**Goal:** build momentum, start paid acquisition, open the commercial pipeline.

- [ ] Launch a small **Facebook/Instagram ad** budget ($5–$15/day) targeting homeowners in service cities, using real before/after creative
- [ ] Post a **before/after TikTok/Reel** 2–3×/week (satisfying content travels)
- [ ] Stand up the **referral program**: $20 credit for the referrer *and* the new customer; mention it after every job
- [ ] Saturate the **two adjacent streets** at every completed job (door hangers + "we just cleaned your neighbor's home")
- [ ] Begin **commercial outreach**: one-page rate sheet to 10 property managers / HOAs / restaurants per week (call + email + drop-by)
- [ ] Add **city landing pages** for the top 2–3 markets (SEO)
- [ ] Collect emails; send a simple **monthly tip + offer** newsletter
- [ ] Join **Nextdoor** conversations and local Facebook groups (be helpful, not spammy)

**60-day KPIs:** first paid leads, 20+ reviews, 1–3 commercial proposals out, referral program live.

---

## 90-Day Marketing Plan

**Goal:** systematize lead flow and lock in recurring revenue.

- [ ] **Realtor partnerships**: pre-listing cleaning packages; pitch 5–10 local agents
- [ ] **Property managers / HOAs**: convert proposals into recurring monthly/quarterly contracts
- [ ] Scale the **ad budget** on the best-performing creative/audience; turn off losers
- [ ] Publish **2 blog posts/month** targeting local keywords; share to social
- [ ] Run a **seasonal promotion** (e.g. pre-summer house wash, pollen-season special)
- [ ] Implement **CRM/scheduling** (Jobber/Housecall Pro) for automated follow-ups, review requests, and invoicing
- [ ] Build an automated **review-request** step into job completion
- [ ] Track **cost-per-lead** and **close rate by channel**; reallocate budget to winners

**90-day KPIs:** 30–40+ reviews, 3–5 recurring commercial contracts, predictable weekly lead flow, positive ROAS on ads.

---

## Local Partnerships & Networking

- **Realtors** — pre-listing curb-appeal packages; offer agent referral perks.
- **Property managers** — portfolio maintenance contracts; reliability sells.
- **HOAs** — common areas, sidewalks, entry monuments on quarterly schedules.
- **Local networking** — Chamber of Commerce, BNI-style groups, community events.
- **Complementary trades** — landscapers, roofers, painters, real estate photographers (cross-referrals).

---

## Referral Program

- **$20 credit** to the referring customer **and** $20 off for the new customer's first service.
- Mention it verbally at job completion, in the thank-you text, and on invoices.
- Track referrals in your CRM/spreadsheet and reward promptly.

---

## Content Engine (repeatable weekly)

1. Shoot before/after at every job (same angle).
2. Post the best pair to Instagram/Facebook + a Reel/TikTok.
3. Turn the same photo into a Google Post and a yard-sign-worthy testimonial.
4. Once a month, expand a common question into a blog post.

> One job → 4+ pieces of marketing. The vehicle wrap and consistent before/after content compound over time.
